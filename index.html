About Me

I'm an electrical engineer passionate about designing systems that connect technology with purpose—whether through satellite control, industrial automation, or IoT innovation. This portfolio reflects my dedication to building reliable, thoughtful solutions that not only function well but also contribute meaningfully to the future.
